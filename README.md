# Online-Book-Store-SQL-Project-PostgreSQL
PostgreSQL Portfolio Project for Data Analyst Resume | Analyzed Online Bookstore data (Books, Customers, Orders) using 20 business questions


#Database Details:

Database Name: OnlineBookstore
Tables: Books.csv, Customers.csv, Orders.csv
ER Diagram me 3 tables ka relation: Customers (1) -> Orders (Many), Books (1) -> Orders (Many)



#solve 20 questions
1. Retrieve all books in the "Fiction" genre 
2. Find books published after the year 1950 
3. List all customers from the Canada 
4. List customers who have placed at least 2 orders
5. Find the details of the most expensive book
6. Retrieve all orders where the total amount exceeds $20 7. List all genres available in the Books table 
8. Find the book with the lowest stock 
9. Show orders placed in November 2023 
10. Retrieve the total quantity of books sold by each author
11. Retrieve the total number of books sold for each genre
12. Calculate the total revenue generated from all orders
13. List the cities where customers who spent over $30 are located 14. Find the average price of books in the "Fantasy" genre
15. Find the most frequently ordered book 
16. Show all customers who ordered more than 1 quantity of a book
17. Retrieve the total stock of books available 
18. Show the top 3 most expensive books of 'Fantasy' Genre
19. Find the top 3 customers who spent the most on orders
20. Calculate the stock remaining after fulfilling all orders
